STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Common questions

Does this replace our EBR, eQMS, or MES?+
No. DataDives connects to what you already run and assembles the evidence for review. It doesn't replace your platform, and the release decision stays with your QA team.
We're still on paper and Excel — is that a problem?+
That's exactly who this is built for. If review still runs through manual logs or spreadsheets, DataDives removes the chasing — it doesn't add a system to learn.
How long does a pilot take?+
A pilot is scoped to one plant and one agreed workflow — small enough to prove review-prep time and outstanding-record age before any wider rollout.
What does it cost?+
It depends on scope — sources, checks, plant complexity. We confirm it on the call, after we understand your workflow, not before.
What happens to our data?+
Used only to confirm fit and follow up about a pilot. Never sold or shared. You can ask us to delete it at any time.
